Local-only historical simulation · split-adjusted Alpaca IEX daily bars

Configuration override

Edit the JSON for this run. Save persistent defaults in backtest.config.json; the local cache is stored separately.

Configuration fields, choices, and units
  • strategy.setups: choose any combination of Setup A, Setup B, and Setup C. A/B are close-confirmed right-side entries; C is an intraday support touch. strategy.watchlist is a list of 1–200 unique uppercase ticker symbols; SPY is added automatically for the regime filter.
  • dateWindow.start and dateWindow.end: ISO dates in YYYY-MM-DD form. The end may be today (New York date); an in-progress daily bar is excluded until 4 p.m. New York time. The cache fetches 600 calendar days before the start and requires at least 300 prior daily bars.
  • portfolio.startingCapital and portfolio.sizing.value are USD amounts unless noted. maxOpenPositions is a count. Sizing types: risk-percent risks that percent of current equity at the initial stop; fixed-dollar sets a per-position notional target; equity-percent sets notional as a percent of current equity. Entries are capped by available cash.
  • stops.initial.type: swing-low uses the minimum low over lookback daily bars; fixed-percent sets a percent below entry; ema-buffer sets a percent below the chosen ema20, ema50, ema100, or ema200 on the signal day. leftSideFailurePercent sets Setup C's hard stop below touched support; the separate close-confirmed failure check applies only on entry day and the following session.
  • profitTaking.type: fixed-percent is a percent above fill price; risk-multiple is a multiple of the entry-to-initial-stop distance. partialPercent (1–100%) is the share of the original position sold at the target; 100% exits fully.
  • trailingExitEma: disabled, ema50, or ema100. The selected standard exit requires two consecutive daily closes below that EMA. For Setup C, standard exits are armed only after a close above the 50 EMA.
  • costs.slippagePercent is adverse slippage in percent on each side; commissionPerOrder is USD per order. A partial trim is a separate sell order and incurs another commission.
Right-side close signals enter at the next session's open. Setup C's daily high cannot trigger a target on its intraday entry bar because daily OHLC data cannot establish whether the high came before or after the touch. When a stop and target are both crossed in one later bar, the stop is assumed first.
