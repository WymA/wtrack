Entry strategies

V1 entries require SPY above its 50 and 200 EMAs; V2/D requires SPY above its 200 EMA. These switches update this draft only. D uses equal-weight slots and its own exits, without V1 profit targets.

Exit rules

A1/A2/A3 identify exit rule types, not Setup A. The same badges apply across entry strategies. Exit checks continue regardless of SPY's entry filter.

V1: Setups A/B/C

A1 Protective stop

Initial stop-loss and early Setup C support-failure exits use stops. Profit targets are configured separately with profitTaking.

A2 50 EMA exit

Two consecutive closes below the 50 EMA. Enabled by trailingExitEma: "ema50"; Setup C arms this exit only after reclaiming the 50 EMA.

A3 100 EMA exit

Two consecutive closes below the 100 EMA. Enabled by trailingExitEma: "ema100"; Setup C arms this exit only after reclaiming the 50 EMA.

V2: Setup D (automatic exits)

D always uses the rules below, with no V1 profit target. trailingExitEma: "disabled" affects V1 only and does not disable D's exits. EMA and rotation exits fill at the next trading open; hard stops remain active intraday.

A1 D: Hard stop

8% below the actual entry fill by default. Set strategy.momentumStop: "atr" for 2.5x signal-day ATR(14) below the fill instead. Active from entry.

A2 D: Standard trailing

Two consecutive closes below the 50 EMA, until the position first reaches a 25% gain.

A3 D: Winner trailing

Once a session high reaches at least 25% above entry, permanently switch to two consecutive closes below the 100 EMA, even if the gain later falls below 25%.

Rotation D: Friday momentum decay

At Friday's close, exit only when all three hold: at least 15 elapsed trading sessions since entry, a close below the 20 EMA, and a momentum rank outside the watchlist's top 30%. Fills at the next available trading open, allowing for holidays.

Configuration fields, choices, and units
  • strategy.setups: choose any combination of Setup A, Setup B, Setup C, and Setup D. A/B are close-confirmed right-side entries; C is an intraday support touch; D ranks volatility-adjusted watchlist momentum. strategy.watchlist is a list of 1–200 unique uppercase ticker symbols; SPY is added automatically for the regime filter and VOO for the performance benchmark. Rank cutoffs round up, with at least one stock in a nonempty ranked universe.
  • strategy.momentumStop: D only. fixed-percent (default) sets a hard stop 8% below the fill; atr uses 2.5x signal-day ATR(14). ATR uses Wilder smoothing. D uses equal-weight slots (equity / maxOpenPositions), ignores V1 sizing and profit targets, and automatically trails the 50 EMA, permanently widening to the 100 EMA once the session high reaches a 25% gain. Friday rotation requires at least 15 elapsed trading sessions, a close below the 20 EMA and a rank outside the top 30%; the exit fills at the next trading open.
  • dateWindow.start and dateWindow.end: ISO dates in YYYY-MM-DD form. The end may be today (New York date); an in-progress daily bar is excluded until 4 p.m. New York time. The cache fetches 600 calendar days before the start and requires at least 300 prior daily bars.
  • portfolio.startingCapital is USD; maxOpenPositions limits all strategies together. V1 sizing types: risk-percent risks that percent of current equity at the initial stop; fixed-dollar sets a per-position USD notional target; equity-percent sets notional as a percent of current equity. Entries are capped by available cash. When mixed with V1, D's highest-score candidates take priority at the next open.
  • stops.initial.type (V1): swing-low uses the minimum low over lookback daily bars; fixed-percent sets a percent below entry; ema-buffer sets a percent below the chosen ema20, ema50, ema100, or ema200 on the signal day. leftSideFailurePercent sets Setup C's hard stop below touched support; the separate close-confirmed failure check applies only on entry day and the following session.
  • profitTaking.type (V1): fixed-percent is a percent above fill price; risk-multiple is a multiple of the entry-to-initial-stop distance. partialPercent (1–100%) is the share of the original position sold at the target; 100% exits fully.
  • trailingExitEma (V1): disabled, ema50, or ema100. The selected standard exit requires two consecutive daily closes below that EMA. For Setup C, standard exits are armed only after a close above the 50 EMA. This does not disable D's trailing exits.
  • costs.slippagePercent is adverse slippage in percent on each side; commissionPerOrder is USD per order. A partial trim is a separate sell order and incurs another commission.
Right-side close signals enter at the next session's open. Setup C's daily high cannot trigger a target on its intraday entry bar because daily OHLC data cannot establish whether the high came before or after the touch. When a stop and target are both crossed in one later bar, the stop is assumed first.
